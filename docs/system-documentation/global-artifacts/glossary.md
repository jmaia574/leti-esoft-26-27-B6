# Glossary

Terms, Expressions, and Acronyms (TEA) are organized alphabetically.

| ***TEA*** (EN)                          | ***TEA*** (PT)                          | ***Description*** (EN)                                                                                                                             |
|:----------------------------------------|:----------------------------------------|:---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| **Administrator**                       | **Administrador**                       | Person responsible for properly configuring and managing core system information such as test types, parameters, laboratories, and employees.       |
| **API**                                 | **API**                                 | Acronym for _Application Programming Interface_.                                                                                                  |
| **Barcode**                             | **Código de Barras**                    | Unique identifier automatically generated via an external API and attached to a biological sample container for traceability.                      |
| **Barcode Generation API**              | **API de Geração de Códigos de Barras** | External system responsible for automatically generating unique barcodes for collected samples.                                                    |
| **Blood Test**                          | **Análise ao Sangue**                   | Clinical test characterized by measuring several blood parameters such as RBC, WBC, and Platelets (PLT).                                           |
| **Chemical Analysis**                   | **Análise Química**                     | Procedure conducted at the central laboratory to measure parameter values and record their execution timestamps.                                   |
| **Chemical Laboratory**                 | **Laboratório Químico**                 | Centralized laboratory located at headquarters in London where chemical analyses are performed on collected samples.                               |
| **Citizen Card Number**                 | **Número do Cartão de Cidadão**         | National identification number requested by the receptionist to identify a client when registering a test.                                        |
| **Client**                              | **Cliente**                             | Person who presents a lab order, undergoes sample collection, and accesses medical results and diagnostic reports.                                |
| **Clinical Analysis Laboratory**        | **Laboratório de Análises Clínicas**    | Network facility spread across England responsible for interacting with clients and collecting biological samples.                                 |
| **Clinical Chemistry Technologist**     | **Técnico de Química Clínica**          | Laboratory professional responsible for receiving delivered samples at headquarters, performing chemical analyses, and recording results.         |
| **Courier**                             | **Estafeta**                            | Person responsible for daily transporting collected biological samples from clinical analysis laboratories to headquarters.                        |
| **COVID-19 Test**                       | **Teste de COVID-19**                   | Clinical test characterized by measuring a single parameter stating a positive or negative result, performed exclusively by an authorized subset.  |
| **Date of Prescription**                | **Data da Prescrição**                  | Date on which a medical doctor prescribes a test, recorded as part of the registered test data.                                                    |
| **Diagnosis**                           | **Diagnóstico**                         | Medical conclusion formulated by a specialist doctor after evaluating chemical analysis results.                                                   |
| **Employee**                            | **Funcionário**                         | General term for UK Labs personnel performing operational duties in the system; the Administrator is modeled as a separate role.                   |
| **Lab Order**                           | **Requisição Médica**                   | Medical prescription provided by a doctor specifying the required test type and the parameters to be measured.                                    |
| **Medical Lab Technician**              | **Técnico de Laboratório Médico**       | Healthcare professional responsible for collecting biological samples from clients and logging them in the system.                                |
| **National Healthcare Service**         | **Serviço Nacional de Saúde**           | Public healthcare system in England referenced in test specifications and client registrations.                                                    |
| **NHS**                                 | **SNS**                                 | Acronym for _National Healthcare Service_.                                                                                                         |
| **NHS Number**                          | **Número do NHS**                       | National Healthcare Service unique identifier requested during client registration.                                                                |
| **Notification**                        | **Notificação**                         | Automated SMS or email alert sent to inform a client that their test results and diagnostic report are available.                                 |
| **Parameter**                           | **Parâmetro**                           | Biological or chemical metric measured during a test, defined by a code, name, description, metric, and optional reference range.                  |
| **Parameter Result**                    | **Resultado de Parâmetro**              | Value obtained for a specific parameter during chemical analysis, recorded with date and execution time.                                          |
| **Platelets (PLT)**                     | **Plaquetas**                           | Parameter measured in blood tests to quantify blood cell fragments involved in clotting.                                                           |
| **Project Manager**                     | **Gestor de Projeto**                   | Role responsible for capturing system-wide requirements and managing project deliverables across sprints.                                          |
| **Receptionist**                        | **Rececionista**                        | Staff member responsible for registering clients and entering prescribed tests into the application.                                              |
| **Red Blood Cells (RBC)**               | **Glóbulos Vermelhos**                  | Blood test parameter quantifying red blood cell count.                                                                                             |
| **Reference Range**                     | **Intervalo de Referência**             | Acceptable minimum and maximum limit values defined for a measurable test parameter.                                                               |
| **Registered Test**                     | **Teste Registado**                     | Instance of a clinical test registered in the application, associating a client, prescription date, collection date, and laboratory.              |
| **Report**                              | **Relatório**                           | Official medical document containing test results and diagnosis, prepared and validated by a specialist doctor.                                   |
| **Sample**                              | **Amostra**                             | Biological specimen collected from a client (e.g., blood, urine, swab) and identified by a unique barcode.                                        |
| **Sample Collection**                   | **Recolha de Amostras**                 | Procedure where a medical lab technician collects specimens from a client and logs them against a registered test.                                 |
| **Specialist Doctor**                   | **Médico Especialista**                 | Medical doctor who examines chemical analysis results, formulates a diagnosis, and prepares the final report.                                      |
| **Staff Member**                        | **Membro da Equipa**                    | Generalized actor representing all internal UK Labs employees who interact with the system.                                                       |
| **Tax Identification Number**           | **Número de Identificação Fiscal**      | Tax identification number collected during client registration and used to sort results.                                                            |
| **Test**                                | **Teste / Análise**                     | Clinical procedure requested by a client, encompassing sample collection, chemical analysis, results, and a medical report.                        |
| **Test Type**                           | **Tipo de Teste**                       | Catalogue definition of a test characterized by an internal code, NHS code, and sample collection method description.                              |
| **Test Type Parameter**                 | **Parâmetro do Tipo de Teste**          | Association linking a test type to one of its parameters; registered tests refer to the parameters specified in their lab orders.                 |
| **TIF**                                 | **NIF**                                 | Acronym for _Tax Identification Number_.                                                                                                           |
| **UK Labs**                             | **UK Labs**                             | English company operating a network of clinical analysis laboratories and a central chemical laboratory in London.                                |
| **UKLabsMS**                            | **Sistema de Gestão UK Labs**           | Acronym for _UK Labs Management System_, the application managing clinical analysis processes.                                                    |
| **White Blood Cells (WBC)**             | **Glóbulos Brancos**                    | Blood test parameter quantifying white blood cell count.                                                                                           |
