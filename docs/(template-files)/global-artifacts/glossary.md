# Glossary

**Terms, Expressions and Acronyms (TEA) must be organized alphabetically.**

Column "TEA (PT)" is optional. If the team does not intend to use it, then remove it.

| ***TEA*** (EN)                      | ***TEA*** (PT)                        | ***Description*** (EN)                                                                                                     |
|:------------------------------------|:--------------------------------------|:---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| **UK Labs**                         | **UK Labs**                           | English company operating a network of clinical analysis laboratories and a central chemical laboratory in London.         |
| **UKLabsMS**                        | **Sistema de Gestão UK Labs**         | Acronym for _UK Labs Management System_, the central application designed to manage clinical analyses across laboratories. |
| **Clinical Analysis Laboratory**    | **Laboratório de Análises Clínicas**  | Local facility in the network responsible for interacting with clients and collecting samples.                             |
| **Chemical Laboratory**             | **Laboratório Químico**               | Centralized laboratory located at headquarters where chemical analyses are performed.                                      |
| **Administrator**                   | **Administrador**                     | Person responsible for configuring core information such as test types, parameters, laboratories, and employees.           |
| **ADM**                             | **ADM**                               | Acronym for _Administrator_.                                                                                               |
| **Employee**                        | **Funcionário**                       | Staff member working at UK Labs carrying out assigned duties within the system.                                            |
| **Receptionist**                    | **Rececionista**                      | Person responsible for registering clients and entering prescribed lab orders.                                             |
| **REC**                             | **REC**                               | Acronym for _Receptionist_.                                                                                                |
| **Medical Lab Technician**          | **Técnico de Laboratório Médico**     | Healthcare professional responsible for collecting biological samples and registering them in the system.                  |
| **MLT**                             | **TLM**                               | Acronym for _Medical Lab Technician_.                                                                                      |
| **Clinical Chemistry Technologist** | **Técnico de Química Clínica**        | Person responsible for receiving samples at headquarters, performing chemical analyses, and recording results.             |
| **CCT**                             | **TQC**                               | Acronym for _Clinical Chemistry Technologist_.                                                                             |
| **Specialist Doctor**               | **Médico Especialista**               | Medical doctor who examines chemical analysis results, formulates a diagnosis, and writes the report.                      |
| **DOC**                             | **MED**                               | Acronym for _Specialist Doctor_.                                                                                           |
| **Client**                          | **Cliente**                           | Person who presents a lab order, undergoes sample collection, and accesses medical results.                                |
| **CLT**                             | **CLT**                               | Acronym for _Client_.                                                                                                      |
| **Citizen Card Number**             | **Número do Cartão de Cidadão**       | Identification number requested by the receptionist to identify a client when registering a test.                          |
| **National Healthcare Service**     | **Serviço Nacional de Saúde**         | Public healthcare system referenced in test specifications and client registrations.                                       |
| **NHS**                             | **SNS**                               | Acronym for _National Healthcare Service_.                                                                                 |
| **NHS Number**                      | **Número do NHS**                     | National Healthcare Service identification number requested during client registration.                                    |
| **Tax Identification Number**       | **Número de Identificação Fiscal**    | Tax identification number collected during client registration and used to sort results.                                   |
| **TIF**                             | **NIF**                               | Acronym for _Tax Identification Number_.                                                                                   |
| **Lab Order**                       | **Requisição Médica**                 | Medical prescription provided by a doctor specifying the required test types and parameters.                               |
| **Date of Prescription**            | **Data da Prescrição**                | Date on which a doctor prescribes a test, recorded as part of the registered test data.                                    |
| **Test**                            | **Teste / Análise**                   | Clinical procedure performed for a client, associated with collected samples, results, and a report.                       |
| **Registered Test**                 | **Teste Registado**                   | Test registered in the system associating a client, prescription date, sample collection date, and laboratory.             |
| **Test Type**                       | **Tipo de Teste**                     | Definition of a test characterized by an internal code, an NHS code, and a collection method description.                  |
| **Blood Test**                      | **Análise ao Sangue**                 | Clinical test characterized by measuring several parameters such as RBC, WBC, and Platelets.                               |
| **COVID-19 Test**                   | **Teste de COVID-19**                 | Clinical test characterized by a single parameter stating a positive or negative result.                                   |
| **Parameter**                       | **Parâmetro**                         | Biological or chemical metric measured during a test, characterized by a code, name, metric, and reference values.         |
| **Reference Values**                | **Valores de Referência**             | Minimum and maximum acceptable limits defined for a test parameter.                                                        |
| **Sample**                          | **Amostra**                           | Biological specimen collected from a client, such as blood, urine, or a swab.                                              |
| **Sample Collection**               | **Recolha de Amostras**               | Process in which a medical lab technician collects biological specimens and logs them in the system.                       |
| **Barcode**                         | **Código de Barras**                  | Identifier generated via an external API and attached to a sample container for tracking.                                  |
| **Courier**                         | **Estafeta**                          | Person responsible for daily transporting collected samples from clinical laboratories to headquarters.                    |
| **Chemical Analysis**               | **Análise Química**                   | Procedure conducted at headquarters to measure parameter values and record their execution timestamps.                     |
| **Parameter Result**                | **Resultado de Parâmetro**            | Value obtained for a specific parameter during chemical analysis, recorded with date and time.                             |
| **Diagnosis**                       | **Diagnóstico**                       | Medical evaluation formulated by a specialist doctor after reviewing chemical analysis results.                            |
| **Report**                          | **Relatório**                         | Official medical document containing test results and diagnosis, prepared by a specialist doctor.                          |
| **Notification**                    | **Notificação**                       | SMS or email alert sent to inform a client that their results and report are available.                                    |
| **Project Manager**                 | **Gestor de Projeto**                 | Role responsible for capturing system-wide requirements and development process tasks.                                     |
| **PM**                              | **GP**                                | Acronym for _Project Manager_.                                                                                             |
